Website Privacy Notice

Information ECS May Collect

When a person contacts ECS by email or telephone, ECS may receive limited business-contact information and the general business inquiry that person chooses to provide.

Do Not Submit Sensitive Information

Do not submit employee names, detailed allegations, complaint files, medical information, Social Security numbers, financial account information, privileged legal communications, passwords, credentials, or other sensitive records through a general website form.

How Information May Be Used

Approved business-contact information may be used to respond to inquiries, assess whether an ECS service may be relevant, maintain basic business records, and protect the security and integrity of ECS systems.

Service Providers

ECS may use an approved website host, email provider, or security service. The controlling providers, their data practices, and any cross-border processing must be documented before activation.

Retention

ECS should retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary for the business purpose, legal obligations, dispute protection, and security needs. A specific retention schedule must be approved before live intake begins.

Security

ECS will use reasonable administrative, technical, and operational safeguards appropriate to the limited information collected. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Legal and Employment Matters

A website inquiry does not create an attorney-client relationship, confidential legal relationship, employment relationship, or advisory engagement. Do not use the website for urgent legal deadlines, emergency reporting, internal complaints, or protected disclosures.

Contact and Updates

The public privacy-contact channel is Contact@executiveculturesolutions.com. This notice may be updated periodically. Material revisions will be reflected on this page.
