Information ECS May Receive

Executive Culture Solutions LLC ("ECS") currently accepts general website-related inquiries through the published business email addresses and telephone number. ECS may receive the business-contact information, message content, and other information a person chooses to provide through those channels.

The reviewed website build does not include a public contact form, payment processor, CRM, scheduling service, advertising tracker, or analytics script. A website host, content-delivery provider, security service, email provider, or telecommunications provider may nevertheless generate ordinary technical records such as IP addresses, request timestamps, security events, delivery records, and service logs as part of operating its service.

Do Not Submit Sensitive Information

Do not send employee names, detailed allegations, complaint files, medical information, Social Security numbers, financial account information, privileged legal communications, passwords, credentials, protected disclosures, or other sensitive records through the public website contact channels unless ECS has specifically provided an approved secure method for that information.

How Information May Be Used

ECS may use ordinary business-contact and inquiry information to respond to inquiries, assess whether an ECS service may be relevant, communicate about a potential or active engagement, maintain appropriate business records, operate and secure ECS systems, prevent misuse, and protect ECS rights and interests.

Service Providers

ECS may use service providers for website hosting, email, telecommunications, security, file storage, and related business operations. Those providers may process limited information needed to provide their services. ECS does not represent that ordinary internet or email communications are completely private or secure.

Retention

ECS may retain ordinary inquiry and business-contact information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry, manage business records, administer an engagement, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, satisfy legal or security obligations, or protect ECS systems and rights. Information may be deleted or de-identified when it is no longer reasonably needed for those purposes.

Security

ECS uses reasonable administrative, technical, and operational safeguards appropriate to the information and systems involved. No website, email service, telephone system, storage provider, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Legal, Employment, and Emergency Matters

A website inquiry does not create an attorney-client relationship, privileged legal relationship, employment relationship, fiduciary relationship, or ECS advisory engagement. Do not use the website contact channels for urgent legal deadlines, emergency reporting, internal employee complaints, whistleblower or protected disclosures, or emergency medical or safety matters.

Your Choices

You may choose not to send ECS information through the public website contact channels. If ECS requests sensitive engagement information, use only the transmission method ECS designates for that purpose.

Contact and Updates

The public privacy-contact channel is Contact@executiveculturesolutions.com. ECS may update this notice as website functionality, providers, or business practices change. Material revisions will be reflected by the revised date on this page.
